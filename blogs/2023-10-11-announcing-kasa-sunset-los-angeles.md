We’re delighted to introduce Kasa Sunset Los Angeles, our newest property located at 5825 Sunset Boulevard. We will begin welcoming guests in December 2023. Kasa Sunset Los Angeles, a 12-story, 55,000-square-foot asset, was originally built as a hotel and converted to an all-studio aparthotel in 2005.
